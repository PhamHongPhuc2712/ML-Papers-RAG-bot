# Chat turn simplification — 2026-10-22

The fixed `condense → retrieve → generate` turn now runs in `chat/pipeline.py`. Serving and the follow-up evaluation share that implementation. The retriever returns `Evidence` directly; the `Document` conversion and LangGraph graph are gone. The structured `ChatOpenAI` generator, spend ledger, one-search rule, stored identity, and SSE contract remain.

## Verification

Before and after the change, the deterministic retrieval smoke benchmark passed with identical metrics and no ranking changes: BM25 nDCG@10 0.962054, dense 0.317653, hybrid 0.730715, hybrid plus rerank 0.967669. The QA smoke benchmark also passed unchanged: accuracy 0.6 over six cases, cost $0, no failures. These fixture checks establish harness and contract parity; they do not measure a quality improvement from the refactor.

The post-change full backend suite passed (803 tests in 12m08s). Two focused cases were added after that run started; its final chat integration rerun passed all 10 tests, and its final pipeline/evaluation unit rerun passed all 22 tests. The offline suite passed (521 tests; 284 integration tests deselected). Strict mypy reported no issues in 91 source files, and Ruff checks and formatting checks on the changed files passed. Frontend lint and typecheck passed, as did 35 Vitest tests and 50 Playwright browser tests. Focused unit tests cover the step order, exactly one search, direct evidence, first-turn and disabled condensing, fallback costs and warnings, spend-cap propagation, empty-evidence abstention, generation validation, references, and timings. The integration tests cover SSE order, persistence, replay, failure, ownership, limits, and disconnects.
