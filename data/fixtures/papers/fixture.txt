Reliable Retrieval for Research Copilots
Ada Lovelace and Grace Hopper
Abstract
We study how a research copilot can retrieve papers reliably and cite only
evidence that exists. This fixture text is original, written for this
repository, and released under CC0 so the parser suite can run offline.
1 Introduction
Research assistants must cite evidence that exists. We describe a small
pipeline that parses documents into sections and splits sections into
chunks that never cross a heading boundary.
---page---
2 Method
The parser splits each page into lines, detects headings, and assigns every
line to the most recent heading. Page spans are recorded from the pages a
section actually touches. Unknown pages remain null rather than invented.

3 Results
The fixture parses into an abstract, three numbered sections, one table and
one reference list. Rerunning the parser on identical bytes reproduces the
same sections, checksums and chunk identifiers.
